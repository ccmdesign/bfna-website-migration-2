# 2020 Senate Races Overshadowed by Trump

_Published: 2019-09-19_

_Authors: Anthony T. Silberfeld_

In November 2020, 21 Republican-held and 12 Democratic-held seats will be up for grabs, but incumbents have already spent the 116th Congress positioning themselves as staunch supporters or vocal critics of the Trump agenda to rally the faithful. With a long campaign ahead, this B|Visual takes a look at the impact of the long shadow the President casts on the upcoming races.
