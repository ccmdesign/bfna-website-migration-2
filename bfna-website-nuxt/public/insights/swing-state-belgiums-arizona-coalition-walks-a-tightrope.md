# Swing State? Belgium’s Arizona Coalition Walks a Tightrope

_Published: 2025-06-02_

_Authors: Zachary Stoor_

Surprising, only because it was [expected](https://www.theguardian.com/world/article/2024/jun/08/belgium-general-election-european-elections-vlaams-belang) to place first. Instead, the right-wing Flemish nationalist party [Nieuw Vlaamse Alliantie](https://resultatselection.belgium.be/fr/election-results/chambre-des-repr%C3%A9sentants/2024/royaume/251712) (N-VA) won and relegated the VB to the opposition. Migration policy and balancing the national budget [dominated](https://www.osce.org/files/f/documents/7/1/569376.pdf) voters’ concerns in the election. The new federal governing coalition, formed in late January after months of inconclusive negotiations, reflects a general rightward shift in Belgian politics. It comprises five parties: the N-VA, which heads the coalition, the liberal Mouvement Réformateur (MR), the center-right Christen-Democratisch en Vlaams (CD&V), the social democratic Vooruit and centrists Les Engagés. Deemed the Arizona coalition for its parties’ shared colors with the U.S. state’s flag, the grouping represents a departure from its predecessor’s centrist politics. The [coalition agreement](https://www.liguedh.be/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Accord-de-majorite%CC%81-ARIZONA-FR-DEFINITIVE.pdf) echoes the topics featured throughout the campaign: curbing migration and balancing the budget, [mainly through welfare cuts](https://www.lalibre.be/belgique/politique-belge/2025/04/11/le-gouvernement-de-wever-sest-accorde-sur-le-budget-2025-la-loi-programme-ainsi-quune-serie-delements-lies-a-la-defense-a-la-justice-et-a-lasile-UGDBSELQANEWDBVHLZL553ZDUI/). Even after navigating a difficult negotiation period, Prime Minister Bart de Wever of the N-VA continues to face significant challenges from the VB. That party’s rise has been steady. It [garnered 22.8% of the vote](https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2024/06/09/right-wing-new-flemish-alliance-wins-belgian-election) in Flanders, lower than the 24%-26% that polls expected but still an increase of three percentage points over its 2019 results. The gains are also playing out elsewhere. In last October’s local elections, the [VB achieved its first mayorship](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/en/2024/12/06/guy-d-haeseleer-becomes-flanders-first-ever-far-right-mayor/) in the small town of Ninove. Perhaps more crucially, the long-standing agreement among all other major parties to refrain from coalitions with the VB was shattered when it entered into governing partnerships in [three](https://www.lalibre.be/belgique/elections-belges/elections-communales/2024/11/26/communales-2024-la-liste-locale-nu2960-rompt-le-cordon-sanitaire-et-conclut-une-coalition-avec-le-vlaams-belang-XPDYGOUJU5B7VIG7N2FY3PZJEY/) Flemish municipalities. A [recent poll](https://www.brusselstimes.com/1487649/vlaams-belang-tops-new-poll-open-vld-at-historical-low) shows the VB already regaining its pre-election popularity, narrowly reclaiming the status of Flanders’ largest party. The Arizona government, therefore, faces a dilemma. With an agreement on migration policy, coalition members are attempting to siphon off votes from the far right by adopting some of its policies. Indeed, Vooruit, the CD&V and the N-VA were among the parties that [shifted furthest to the right on migration](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2024/04/29/de-stemtest-leert-vooruit-en-cd-v-zijn-op-vlak-van-migratie-het/) ahead of June’s vote. Vooruit’s [proposals included restrictions](https://www.hln.be/binnenland/experts-niet-mals-voor-migratieplannen-vooruit-doel-is-om-extreemrechts-publiek-aan-te-trekken~a2d044d2/?fbclid=IwAR3mNoSqGNiYZba5ap6yKVRjHk2s9p8Zj-POIa85bDKLPt1w84yLVjPJjz8&referrer=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.google.com%2F) on family reunification and automatic rejection of asylum applications from countries deemed “safe”. Other parties made similar moves.
