# What is Gerrymandering?

_Published: 2020-09-09_

_Authors: Anthony T. Silberfeld_

With the United States gearing up for the 2020 General Election, the Bertelsmann Foundation has created a three-part animated series highlighting key factors that have contributed to the erosion of American democracy. ![Elbridge Gerry's grave](//images.ctfassets.net/9vgcz0fppkl3/2Op5Q4h9c896Ipq1glHPeR/cb4cb02f404ac4ca69e25e6d55f020ae/vlcsnap-2020-09-22-14h05m13s175.png) Our first episode tackles the topic of Gerrymandering and answers these important questions: How did this practice begin? How does it work? What is its impact on voter participation? How can it be defeated? This animation is part of the Bertelsmann Foundation’s [collection of educational videos](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL36lfVkdVQqkfgG4Qn0DeVm3OD3xrfJrS) focused on critical issues in the United States and the European Union.
