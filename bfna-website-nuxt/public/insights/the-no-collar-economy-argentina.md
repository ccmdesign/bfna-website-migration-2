# The No Collar Economy: Argentina

_Published: 2018-07-19_

_Authors: Samuel George_

From the soy farmers of La Pampa, to the Uber drivers of Buenos Aires, digital tools are changing the future of work in Argentina, and the lessons learned will reverberate throughout the Americas. Filmed and directed by the Bertelsmann Foundation’s Samuel George, this documentary follows Argentina’s development and disruption in the global digital revolution. This is the No Collar Economy – Let’s go inside.
