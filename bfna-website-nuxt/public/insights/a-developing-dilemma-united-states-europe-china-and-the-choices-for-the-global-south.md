# A Developing Dilemma: United States, Europe, China, and the Choices for the Global South

_Published: 2023-04-27_

This animation explores the various ways in which the transatlantic relationship can appeal to countries in the global south. Digital cooperation, economic exchange, and diaspora engagement are all examples of ways in which transatlantic partners can engage with the developing world. This animation is part of the 2022 Bertelsmann Foundation Fellowship. For additional information on the subject, read the Fellowship publication: [*Emerging Ties: Essays on Transatlantic Engagement in the Developing World*](https://www.bfna.org/politics-society/emerging-ties/)
