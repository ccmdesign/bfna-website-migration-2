# Transponder Issue #7: The Future

_Published: 2025-05-23_

The seventh issue of Transponder Magazine explores the critical choices shaping the future of our world, from the geopolitics of space and the race for rare earth minerals to the rise of AI and its societal impact. Global perspectives include young voices in Taiwan and South Korea, a deep dive into France’s democratic aspirations, and historical memory’s role in the Balkans’ EU accession. The challenges and opportunities ahead are vast. The future is in our hands—how will we shape it?
