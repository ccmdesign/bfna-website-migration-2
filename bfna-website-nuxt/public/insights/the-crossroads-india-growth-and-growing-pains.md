# The Crossroads India: Growth and Growing Pains

_Published: 2017-09-14_

_Authors: Samuel George_

![000](//images.ctfassets.net/9vgcz0fppkl3/68WrT73MFplLEehHj1h0b0/0db8081e8d4034d02ab8fecebf1122c1/000.png) India has emerged as a global leader in growth. But has it been growth for all? If not, why not? And could the country’s gamble on digital technology help close the gap? This new film by Samuel George of the Bertelsmann Foundation dives into these complex questions.From the heights of Mumbai to remote tribal lands near the Pakistani border, this is the Crossroads India - let’s go inside. The documentary is the latest edition of the Crossroads series, a collaboration of the Global Economic Dynamics project of the Bertelsmann Stiftung and the Bertelsmann Foundation North America.
