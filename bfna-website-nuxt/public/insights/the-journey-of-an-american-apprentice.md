# The Journey of An American Apprentice

_Published: 2020-11-12_

_Authors: Brandon Bohrn_

Of the many areas of German-American cooperation, one has stood out above the rest in recent years: the export of German-style apprenticeships to the United States. In our latest animation, created in partnership with the [Transatlantic Outreach Program](https://www.goethe.de/ins/us/en/spr/eng/top.html), we follow the story of Gage, a current engineer and former apprentice in the southeastern U.S. During his apprenticeship, Gage became qualified for the jobs of the future, received competitive wages, earned college degrees, and built a solid foundation for career advancement at a company that believed in him. In his words, it was the turning point that changed his life for the better. This animation is part of the [Wunderbar Together 2020](https://www.wunderbar2gether.org/home) initiative that aims to promote the importance of robust German-American relations.
