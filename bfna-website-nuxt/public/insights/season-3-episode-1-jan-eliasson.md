# Season 3, Episode 1 | Jan Eliasson

_Published: 2024-02-13_

The Bertelsmann Foundation and Aspen Institute are pleased to announce the premiere of Season Three of the Leadership in Action series.

In episode one, Jan Eliasson, is a Swedish diplomat and former Deputy Secretary-General of the United Nations, takes us through key moments of leadership in his career of diplomacy and peace keeping.

This is his story, in his own words.
