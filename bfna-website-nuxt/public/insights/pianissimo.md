# Pianissimo:

_Published: 2025-04-07_

_Authors: Zachary Stoor_

In a shock for pro-EU forces, the [self-proclaimed admirer of Benito Mussolini](https://www.bbc.com/news/world-europe-63351655) and leader of a party with neofascist roots has turned out to be [less of a bomb-thrower than expected](https://www.dw.com/en/italys-giorgia-meloni-not-so-radical-after-all/a-66901456) at the European level. Domestically, though, her populist politics run deep. Since her 2022 election, Meloni has overseen a campaign against press freedom. She has frequently [sued journalists](https://www.irishtimes.com/world/europe/2024/08/12/giorgia-melonis-thin-skinned-government-cause-concern-for-press-freedom-in-italy/) who criticize her and [eroded the independence of state broadcaster RAI](https://www.repubblica.it/politica/2024/10/15/news/liberta_stampa_governo_meloni_reuters_institute_oxford-423556807/). She is also [accused](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2025/feb/19/journalists-launch-legal-action-against-italian-government-over-spyware-claims) of using [spyware](https://www.lemonde.fr/en/international/article/2025/02/24/giorgia-meloni-s-government-in-italy-targets-press-and-tightens-grip-on-public-broadcasting_6738483_4.html) against investigative journalists. Her actions, however, are not confined to the media. In response to multiple [court rulings that blocked her migration policies](https://www.politico.eu/article/giorgia-melonis-albania-migration-plan-blasted-after-third-failure/) for human rights violations, she has [publicly attacked judges](https://www.politico.eu/article/giorgia-melonis-italy-judges-viktor-orban-hungary-albania-rome-court-russia-war-ukraine/) and launched a [constitutional reform process](https://fr.boell.org/it/2025/02/20/unione-dei-poteri-come-il-governo-meloni-vorrebbe-controllare-il-potere-giudiziario) that would weaken the judiciary’s independence. On top of that, she [has moved to amend](https://www.rainews.it/articoli/2024/05/elezione-diretta-del-premier-convegno-costituzione-di-tutti-diaologo-sul-premierato-giorgia-meloni-7db1f9b1-06d1-4cad-a93c-c7ae6f7ee7d3.html) the Italian constitution to [automatically give the top party in each national election](https://www.lemonde.fr/en/international/article/2024/06/29/giorgia-meloni-wants-to-transform-italian-democracy_6676105_4.html) a legislative majority, no matter how small the margin. The amendment would also [strip the president of the authority to dissolve parliament](https://verfassungsblog.de/italy-constitutional-reform-meloni-president/) and name prime ministers. Combined, these changes would cement minority rule and remove most checks on executive power. The European Commission has been mostly silent about [Rome’s democratic erosion](https://www.idea.int/democracytracker/country/italy). While the [2024 Commission Rule of Law Report](https://commission.europa.eu/document/download/60d79a4f-49cd-4061-a18f-d3a4495d6485_en?filename=29_1_58066_coun_chap_italy_en.pdf) did criticize Italy’s [declining media freedom](https://www.euronews.com/2024/07/25/italian-government-rejects-eu-commission-warning-about-interference-in-public-media), the country has not faced the same [threats of funding freezes or Council voting restrictions](https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2024/01/29/eu-commission-wont-trigger-nuclear-option-against-hungary-until-theres-clear-majority-in-f) that [Slovakia](https://www.dw.com/en/slovakia-seemingly-unfazed-by-eu-threat-to-withhold-funding/a-70277869) and [Hungary](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2022/12/12/rule-of-law-conditionality-mechanism/) have for their backsliding. In fact, Commission President Ursula von der Leyen was [accused of delaying the release of the report](https://www.politico.eu/article/ursula-von-der-leyen-italy-rome-commission-president-giorgia-meloni-elections/) to avoid antagonizing Meloni ahead of the 2024 European Parliament elections.
