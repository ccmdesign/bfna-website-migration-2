# The Ukraine Crisis: Which countries are accepting refugees within the transatlantic community?

_Published: 2023-01-18_

_Authors: Courtney Flynn Martino, Brandon Bohrn_

Since Russia's invasion of Ukraine in February 2022, over 7.5 million refugees have fled Ukraine's borders. In this infographic, we take a look at which countries featured in the [Transatlantic Barometer](https://www.transatlanticbarometer.org/humanitarian-aid-to-ukraine-and-refugee-intake) are accepting refugees. For more information, visit the [Transatlantic Barometer](https://www.transatlanticbarometer.org/humanitarian-aid-to-ukraine-and-refugee-intake). Source: UNHCR, the UN Refugee Agency
