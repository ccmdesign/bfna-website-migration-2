# Crisis and Consequences

_Published: 2021-04-23_

_Authors: Brandon Bohrn_

Germany’s initial response to the COVID-19 pandemic garnered both domestic and international praise. Subsequent shortcomings have set the country back as it suffers a third wave of infections. Uncertainty during Germany’s pandemic takes place during a Superwahljahr with seven major elections scheduled this year, including federal elections on September 26. This B|Visual offers a glimpse into the political impact of the latest developments in Germany’s crisis response.
