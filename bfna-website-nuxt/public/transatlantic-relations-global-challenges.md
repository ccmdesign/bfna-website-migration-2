# Transatlantic Relations & Global Challenges

The transatlantic community is undergoing profound change. Geopolitical competition, economic shifts, technological disruption, and evolving political landscapes are reshaping the global environment and redefining cooperation between the United States and Europe.

The Bertelsmann Foundation examines the forces shaping transatlantic relations and explores how partners on both sides of the Atlantic can address shared global challenges. Through research, polling, forecasting, interactive tools, and multimedia storytelling, we provide insights into emerging trends, foster informed dialogue, and explore pathways for cooperation in a rapidly changing world.
