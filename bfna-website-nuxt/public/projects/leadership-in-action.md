# Leadership in Action

Now in its fifth season, Leadership in Action explores the experiences, strategies, and lessons of leaders who have navigated some of the world's most complex challenges. Through in-depth conversations with diplomats, policymakers, and practitioners, the series examines how leadership, negotiation, and resilience shape responses to conflict, political change, and global crises.

Previous episodes have featured Jan Eliasson, former Swedish Foreign Minister and Deputy Secretary-General of the United Nations, reflecting on his decades of diplomatic efforts to resolve international conflicts; Ugandan opposition leader Bobi Wine, discussing his journey from political repression to mobilizing a movement for democracy; and Tzipi Livni, former Israeli Foreign Minister, sharing insights from her role as Israel's chief negotiator during peace talks with the Palestinian Authority in the 2000s.

Produced in ongoing collaboration with the Aspen Ministers Forum of the Aspen Institute, Leadership in Action brings together the perspectives of experienced leaders and practitioners to highlight the lessons of diplomacy, negotiation, and leadership in addressing today's global challenges.
