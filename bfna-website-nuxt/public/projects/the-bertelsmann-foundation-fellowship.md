# The Bertelsmann Foundation Fellowship

Building on the legacy of the Congressional European Parliamentary Initiative (CEPI), the Bertelsmann Foundation Fellowship (BFF) is an eight-month hybrid transatlantic fellowship program that connects emerging leaders from North America and Europe to exchange perspectives, build lasting networks, and deepen their understanding of the issues shaping the transatlantic relationship.

Each year, fellows explore a defining global challenge through a combination of virtual discussions with experts, policymakers, and thought leaders, as well as in-person engagements that foster dialogue, collaboration, and relationship-building across the Atlantic and beyond. The program culminates in a collaborative writing project that captures fellows' perspectives and insights on the year's theme.

Following the program, fellows become part of a growing transatlantic alumni network, with continued opportunities to engage with the Bertelsmann Foundation, fellow participants, and future cohorts.
