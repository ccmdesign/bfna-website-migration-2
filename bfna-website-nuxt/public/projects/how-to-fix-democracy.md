# How to Fix Democracy

How to Fix Democracy explores the challenges facing democratic systems and the ideas, innovations, and solutions that can strengthen them. Through conversations with experts, practitioners, and leaders from around the world, the podcast examines how societies can rebuild trust, address polarization, and ensure democracy remains resilient in a changing world.

Hosted by author and writer Andrew Keen, How to Fix Democracy brings together leading thinkers, writers, politicians, technologists, and business leaders to explore the challenges facing democracy and examine ideas for its renewal.

Launched in 2019, the podcast explores democracy's origins, evolution, successes, and shortcomings in the 21st century. Through in-depth conversations, the series examines how democratic institutions and societies can respond to today's challenges and adapt for the future.
