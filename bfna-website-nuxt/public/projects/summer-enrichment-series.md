# Summer Enrichment Series

Launched in 2022, the Summer Enrichment Series (SES) is a Bertelsmann Foundation program that brings together students and young professionals with a strong interest in the transatlantic relationship. Through monthly roundtable discussions in June, July, and August, participants engage with experts and peers on timely issues shaping the transatlantic partnership and exploring the geopolitical developments influencing the alliance.

The series provides an opportunity for the next generation of leaders to exchange perspectives, expand their networks, and deepen their understanding of the challenges and opportunities facing the United States and Europe.
