# BFNA Documentaries

BFNA Documentaries brings the Foundation’s research and reporting to life on screen. Believing in the power of film to tell the critical stories of our complicated times, its films present candid, honest perspectives without projecting an agenda, allowing viewers to draw their own conclusions.

The catalog spans more than 35 films, including the series Democracy in Cities, People in Evidence, and Leadership in Action, with works screened and recognized at festivals internationally.
