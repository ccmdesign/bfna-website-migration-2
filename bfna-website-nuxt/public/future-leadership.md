# Future Leadership

Strong transatlantic leadership has never been more important. As the United States and Europe confront growing geopolitical uncertainty, effective leadership depends on trust, collaboration, and a shared understanding of common interests.

The Bertelsmann Foundation's Future Leadership program brings together current and emerging leaders to strengthen transatlantic cooperation. Through original research, public opinion polling, the Bertelsmann Foundation Fellowship (BFF), and informal convenings of diplomats, policymakers, and future leaders, we foster trusted relationships, encourage informed dialogue, and help shape solutions to the defining challenges of our time.
