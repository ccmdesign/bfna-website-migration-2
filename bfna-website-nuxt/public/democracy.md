# Democracy

Democracies around the world are facing profound challenges, from declining trust in institutions and political polarization to technological disruption and changing social and economic conditions. These pressures are reshaping democratic systems and raising important questions about how democracies can adapt and remain resilient.

The Bertelsmann Foundation examines the forces shaping democracy and explores how societies, institutions, and communities can respond to these challenges. Through research, data-driven analysis, documentary storytelling, and dialogue, our work identifies emerging trends, highlights innovative approaches, and contributes to a deeper understanding of how democracies can evolve and thrive.
