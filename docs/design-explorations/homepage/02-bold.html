Direction 02 — proof sheet

Working title
Loud & Clear (Bold) — type as image. Poster-scale headlines, saturated colour fields, hard-cropped photography.
Typefaces
Bricolage Grotesque variable, 800, opsz 96, tracking −0.04em, leading 0.9 — display.
Inter 400/500/600 at 17–19px — body and UI.
System mono 13px uppercase — metadata.

Palette

Ink
#0A0A0A White
#FFFFFF Teal
#0B8B99 Amber
#F0A82A Red
#E03A4E Navy
#002B44

Principles

  1. Type is the image. Scale and colour do the work; no decoration, no shadow, no radius.
  2. Full-bleed fields with ink type. Large colour alternates with white; never more than three hues in one viewport.
  3. Photography cropped hard and big — one photo per idea, never a supporting illustration.
